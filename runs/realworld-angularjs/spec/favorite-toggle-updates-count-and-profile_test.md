# Favorite and unfavorite an article

## Favorite and unfavorite an article
Starting from the seeded app, sign in with email "alice@example.com" and password "conduit-demo", open "Favorites and counters", choose "Favorite Article (1)", store the visible favorite action text as 'favorite_action', then verify the stored favorite_action value is "Unfavorite Article (2)"; choose "Unfavorite Article (2)", open alice's profile and select "Favorited Articles", store the visible article titles in display order as 'article_titles', then verify the stored article_titles value is "none" and the page shows "No articles are here... yet.".
